About

From numbers to people

An economist by training, a product designer by choice. I spent six years inside the same fintech seeing the product from three different angles: data, content and interface.

I started my career in Economics, graduating from UFSC, drawn to understanding how people make decisions in the face of information, risk and incentives. That curiosity took me into a fintech, and, over time, led me to shift from analyzing numbers to designing the experiences that shape that behavior.

Today I see design not as aesthetics, but as translation: turning business needs, data and human behavior into interfaces that make sense to the people using them.

At Franq, I started as a Product Analyst, tracking metrics and user behavior to support roadmap and prioritization. I then took on the Educational Designer role, turning dense financial topics into clear learning journeys. That is where an interest in interface and behavior became a calling. From 2023 to 2026 I worked as a UX Designer, running projects from discovery to delivery alongside PMs and developers.

I work well with complex products: rule-heavy flows, dense data, financial decisions. I like projects where interface clarity changes the business outcome, and where the design system keeps that clarity alive after delivery.

Design is not aesthetics. It is translation.

How I work

The process changes from project to project, but the sequence repeats.

01Discovery

Understand before designing

Interviews, usage data and benchmarking to separate the real problem from the stated one. I use AI to accelerate reference gathering and cover more ground in less time.

02Structure

Architecture and flow

Journey mapping, information architecture and flows before any pixel. This is where the most expensive decisions are made and where business, technology and user need to align.

03Interface

High-fidelity prototype

Navigable screens built on the design system, with microcopy treated as part of the interface, not as finishing.

04Delivery

Follow it until it ships

Detailed handoff, implementation review with the development team and adjustments based on real behavior after launch.
